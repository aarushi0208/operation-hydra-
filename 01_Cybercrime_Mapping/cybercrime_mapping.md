# Operation Hydra – Cybercrime Decomposition & Mapping

## 1. Introduction

Operation Hydra is a simulated multi-stage cybercrime campaign designed to demonstrate how different types of cyber offences can be combined into a coordinated attack.

The simulated campaign begins with phishing emails and spoofed communication, followed by a fake authentication page, simulated malware delivery, unauthorized account access, and fraudulent financial transactions.

The investigation focuses on identifying the individual cybercrime components, understanding their relationship within the attack chain, and mapping the observed activities to relevant legal frameworks.
